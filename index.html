welcome!

I am a PhD researcher in Finance at Goethe University Frankfurt and a research assistant at the Leibniz Institute for Financial Research SAFE.

My research focuses on household finance and subjective expectations. I study how perceived income uncertainty and intra-household insurance beliefs shape households’ consumption, saving, debt repayment, and portfolio decisions.

Visiting Copenhagen (November 2026 – March 2027). I am currently a visiting PhD researcher at the Department of Economics, University of Copenhagen and the Center for Economic Behavior and Inequality (CEBI) . If you are in Copenhagen during this period, feel free to reach out — I would be happy to connect and exchange research ideas!
